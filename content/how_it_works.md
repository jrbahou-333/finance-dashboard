### What is this?

This dashboard tracks your **net worth**, **cash flow**, and **projections** over time. All the data behind it can be added and edited directly in the app, and is stored locally on your machine.

### Insights

**Net Worth** shows your account balances over time as a stacked area chart, an allocation pie of your current balances, and a table comparing how much you've invested versus the current value of each account. **Cash Flow** breaks down income against recurring and one-off spending, and updates immediately as you log new expenses. **Projection** extrapolates your future net worth from your income and expenses, and automatically fills in "actual" values whenever you log a new net worth snapshot.

### Managing your data

Use the Manage Data pages to add monthly account snapshots, set how much you've invested per account, and log, edit, or delete one-off expenses. These updates feed straight into the charts above — nothing extra to sync.
